Repora Privacy Policy

Private fitness. Your data. Your progress.

The Core Principle: The user owns the data. We don't. Repora does not require an account, has no remote cloud backend, includes zero advertising SDKs, and does not collect or transmit personal fitness or biometric telemetry.

1. Data Collection & Storage

All fitness logs, workout routines, exercise records, body weight measurements, progress photos, and personal settings created within Repora are stored strictly locally on your device using client-side sandboxed storage (IndexedDB / SQLite). No telemetry, tracking identifiers, or usage statistics are collected or shared with any third party.

2. Accounts & Authentication

Repora requires no account creation, no email address, no phone number, and no external login providers. You can use 100% of the application's capabilities anonymously from the moment you install it.

3. Data Ownership & Export

You maintain complete ownership of your fitness records. Repora provides native, one-tap data export and backup tools:

  • Structured JSON Export: Export your full dataset at any time as an unencrypted, human-readable JSON backup file.
  • Safe Backup Restore: Preview backup files, merge records non-destructively, or restore complete databases.
  • Permanent Erase: A one-tap permanent deletion tool is available directly in the Profile settings to permanently wipe all stored data from your device.

4. Camera & Storage Permissions

If you optionally choose to store progress photos, photos are processed entirely on-device and stored in local device storage. Photos are never uploaded to any remote server or scanned by any third-party vision service.

5. Google Play Store Data Safety Declaration

  • Data collected: None.
  • Data shared with third parties: None.
  • Security practices: Data stored locally within the application sandbox. Optional PIN/Biometric app lock is available on-device.
